About

I have never had a perfectly linear career path, and that is exactly what I love about it. I started in content and storytelling before finding myself drawn to the systems, processes, and collaboration that turn ideas into outcomes. That curiosity led me into project management, operations, and business transformation.

Today, I work at the intersection of people, process, and execution. Over the last six years, I have stepped into programs that were genuinely chaotic, ambiguous mandates, teams unsure of what leadership actually wanted, deadlines that assumed clarity nobody had yet. My role has consistently been the same one: find the midpoint between what is being asked for and what is actually possible, and lead the team there with empathy rather than pressure.

I am passionate about continuous improvement, operational excellence, and exploring how AI can help teams work smarter and focus on higher value work. The projects below are less about the tools I used and more about the moments where structure had to be built from scratch, often under pressure, and always with people on the other end of the outcome.
